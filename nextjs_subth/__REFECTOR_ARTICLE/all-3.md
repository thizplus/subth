🧨 Prompt pipeline ที่ควรใช้
🔹 Chunk 1 — Quick Answer
Generate a quick answer box:
- Who is this for?
- Is it worth watching?
- What makes it unique?

Max 3 bullets.
🔹 Chunk 2 — Summary
Write a concise story recap based on transcript.
Focus on progression, not scenes.
🔹 Chunk 3 — Review
Evaluate acting, pacing, mood and production quality.
Write as reviewer, not narrator.
🔹 Chunk 4 — Character insight
Explain personalities and dynamics from dialogue.
🔹 Chunk 5 — FAQ
Generate 3 user questions someone searching this video would ask.
Answer concisely.
🔹 Chunk 6 — Title & Meta
Create:
- high CTR title
- search friendly H1
- meta description
🔹 Chunk 7 — Internal linking
Suggest related tags and videos.
