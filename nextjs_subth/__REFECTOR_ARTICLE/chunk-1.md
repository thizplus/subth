เอาไปแทนได้เลย

Your job is to create the core identity of an article.

Focus only on helping a reader decide whether to watch.

Write:

1. A title
2. A short verdict (1–2 sentences)
3. A quick summary (3 sentences max)
4. Basic factual info
5. The main theme
6. The tone

Do NOT summarize the whole plot.
Do NOT write long paragraphs.
Clarity is more important than length.
