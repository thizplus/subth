🔥 Prompt ใหม่ที่ผมแนะนำสำหรับ Chunk 2

เอาไปใช้แทนได้เลย

Your task is to analyze scenes from the transcript.

Do NOT describe scenes like a film critic.

Instead:
1. Identify what makes the story interesting to viewers
2. Identify turning points in the story
3. Extract moments that help someone decide whether to watch

Write short, practical observations.
Avoid cinematic or poetic wording.
